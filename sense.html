This page is a simulation for the GEW 2026 Green Economy Hackathon. No real meter is connected. The numbers follow real physics: the meter's LED blinks 1600 times per kWh, the Sense clip counts the blinks, and the app works out power, units left and cost at D13.50 per kWh. In the real product, top-up from the phone needs a partnership with NAWEC and a mobile money provider. Made by Team Holal.
